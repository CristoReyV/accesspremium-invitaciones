## Visual QA checklist

- First viewport feels like an invitation, not a landing page.
- Texture and ornaments are visible.
- Countdown is integrated into the theme.
- Location CTA is obvious.
- RSVP exists both in-page and floating where appropriate.
- Mobile view has no clipped headings, overlapping controls, or horizontal overflow.
- Desktop view stays centered as a premium invitation experience.
